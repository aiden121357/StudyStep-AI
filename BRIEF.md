# Project brief: StudyStep AI

## The competition

- Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), national K–12 competition.
- Age group: Middle school (grades 6–8).
- Judges score a written entry (PDF up to 10 pages) and a video (up to 4 minutes).
- Project submission deadline: December 23, 2026, at 5:00pm. Registration deadline: December 18, 2026, at 5:00pm Eastern Time.

## The problem

Students at school struggle with difficult homework because they do not always have someone available to help. Students need explanations and confidence, not just answers.

Evidence: In 2025, 31% of U.S. public school students were behind in at least one subject. Source: https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/spp/results.asp

Student feedback: one student needs help with problems across many subjects; another wants step-by-step breakdowns.

## My solution

StudyStep AI explains homework step by step instead of only showing an answer. It is free, personalized, and works across subjects. Safety plans include checking answers, protecting privacy, and refusing inappropriate or harmful requests.

## MVP features

1. Paste a homework problem and get a step-by-step AI explanation.
2. Pick a subject so the explanation fits the class.
3. Ask the AI to explain one step more simply.
4. Get correct, safe answers to real questions.

## Maybe later

- Try the next step before seeing it.
- Reminder to check answers with a teacher.
- Warning not to type personal information.
- Politely refuse off-topic or rude questions.
- Get a hint instead of a full step.
- Get a similar practice problem.
- Upload a photo of the problem.
- Save explanations to review before a test.

## AI instructions

Help students understand difficult homework by explaining problems step by step across subjects. Use simple, friendly words. Use correct information from school textbooks, class notes, and trusted educational websites. Never make up facts or answers. Never give harmful advice, ask for personal information, or pretend wrong answers are correct. Give short answers that are easy to understand.
